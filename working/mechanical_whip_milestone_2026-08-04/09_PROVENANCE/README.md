# Provenance

`curtain_vs_q_full_console_capture.txt` is the preserved uploaded command-and-console capture associated with the Q-dependent curtain scan.

All other console logs remain beside their corresponding result families. No numerical content has been altered. The only text normalization applied during packaging was the replacement of a small number of Italian working labels in the final rotating-memory and single-whip reports/logs with equivalent English labels, in accordance with the archive language requirement.

The numerical values, file formats, tables, compressed arrays, and figures were not changed. Three rotating-memory figures with Italian working titles were not included; equivalent English-title figures were regenerated directly from the preserved phase-profile CSV.
