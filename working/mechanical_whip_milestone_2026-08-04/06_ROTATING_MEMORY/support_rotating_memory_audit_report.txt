READING GUIDE
-------------
C_line uses the absolute tangent overlap: a direction and its
negative are treated as the same geometric tangent line.

The peak lag is a simple phase-of-maximum reading. The circular
cross-correlation lag uses the complete waveform and is therefore
the more robust staffetta indicator when profiles contain several
local maxima.

The growth curves are densities on the actual Theta rotation.
They are not blurred by the slow drift between Theta and t/T_ref.
