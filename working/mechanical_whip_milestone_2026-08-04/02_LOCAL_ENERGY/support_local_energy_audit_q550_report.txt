READING GUIDE
-------------
P_support = mu_x * rho1 * rho2 * sin(Delta) * x_dot.
A nonzero mean P_support is net work from the rotating support.
A large mean |P_support| with a small mean P_support indicates strong
alternating exchange with weak rectification, not a neutral support.
The transfer-alignment coefficient measures the phase alignment
between activity and x_dot. The phase-bin curves show where in Delta
the support feeds or brakes the local motion.
